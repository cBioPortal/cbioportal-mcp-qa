## IDH1 Mutations in Cancer

**IDH1** (isocitrate dehydrogenase 1) encodes a cytoplasmic enzyme involved in cellular metabolism. In cancer:

1. **Loss of function + gain of abnormal function:** Most IDH1 mutations (especially **R132H**, the most common) are heterozygous and cause:
   - Loss of normal enzymatic activity (reduced α-ketoglutarate production)
   - Gain of new catalytic ability to produce **2-hydroxyglutarate (2-HG)**, an "oncomet"

2. **2-HG accumulation** acts as an epigenetic driver:
   - Inhibits α-ketoglutarate-dependent dioxygenases (TET, JMJC family)
   - Blocks DNA/histone demethylation → hypermethylation phenotype
   - Associated with altered differentiation and cellular transformation

3. **Disease associations:**
   - **Glioma:** IDH1/IDH2 mutations define a molecular subtype with better prognosis than IDH wild-type glioblastoma
   - **Acute myeloid leukemia (AML):** ~7% of AML cases; associated with intermediate prognosis
   - **Cholangiocarcinoma, colorectal, and other cancers:** less frequent
