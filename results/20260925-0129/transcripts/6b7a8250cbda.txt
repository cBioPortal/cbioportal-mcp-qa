**Scale:**
- **897 distinct OncoTree cancer type/subtype codes** — from broad tissue categories down to fine histologic and molecular subtypes (e.g., separate codes for "Astrocytoma, IDH-Mutant, Grade 2" vs. "Grade 3" vs. "Grade 4").
- These codes are organized under a much smaller number of top-level tissue lineages (breast, lung, CNS/brain, hematopoietic, GI, GU, skin, soft tissue, etc.).
