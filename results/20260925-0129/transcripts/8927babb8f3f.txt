**cBioPortal contains 548 studies**, representing a comprehensive collection of cancer genomics datasets spanning:

- **Large consortium projects:** TCGA, ICGC/PCAWG, TARGET (pediatric)
- **Institution-specific cohorts:** MSK-IMPACT and its variants (MSK, 2017–2026), specialized MSK projects (Met Tropism, Clonal Hematopoiesis, CHORD, ctDNA)
- **Disease-specific studies:** single-disease deep dives and multi-patient series
- **Cell line repositories:** CCLE (Broad, multiple versions)
- **International collaborations:** METABRIC (breast), MDS IWG, China Pan-cancer (OrigiMed), GLASS (glioma)

The largest studies by sample count are MSK-IMPACT 50K (~54k samples), Cancer Therapy & Clonal Hematopoiesis 2023 (~43k), and MSK MetTropism (~26k). You can browse all studies at [cBioPortal Studies](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary).
