**Bottom line:** cBioPortal, as queried here, does not host any study with a polygenic risk score. PRS data (typically derived from GWAS summary statistics and germline SNP arrays) isn't a data type cBioPortal is generally built to store — its genomic data model centers on somatic mutations, copy number, expression, and structural variants, plus limited germline variant calls in select studies. If you're looking for PRS data specifically, that would more likely be found in a GWAS-focused resource (e.g., the PGS Catalog) rather than cBioPortal.
