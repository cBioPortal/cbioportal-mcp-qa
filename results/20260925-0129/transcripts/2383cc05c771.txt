Most likely you mean one of:

1. **Lung adenocarcinoma vs. lung squamous cell carcinoma** — the classic NSCLC subtype comparison (TCGA PanCancer Atlas cohorts above), or
2. **Lung squamous cell carcinoma vs. squamous cell carcinoma at a different site** (e.g., head & neck, esophagus, cervix) — if you're asking whether lung SCC's mutation profile differs from SCC elsewhere in the body.
