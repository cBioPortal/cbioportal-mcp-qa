**542 studies** in the cBioPortal database contain mutation data (i.e., have at least one sample profiled for mutations).

This count is based on studies where `mutation_sample_count > 0` in the cancer study metadata table, which tracks the number of samples profiled for somatic mutations across all studies in the portal.
