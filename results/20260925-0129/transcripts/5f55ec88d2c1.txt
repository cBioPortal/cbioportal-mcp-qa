## Renal Cancer Studies with RNA Expression Data

The following **8 renal cancer studies** have RNA expression (mRNA) data available:

### Clear Cell RCC
1. **[Renal Clear Cell Carcinoma (TCGA GDC, 2025)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=ccrcc_tcga_gdc)** — 537 samples
   - mRNA Expression (FPKM, TPM, counts) with z-scores

2. **[Kidney Renal Clear Cell Carcinoma (TCGA, PanCancer Atlas)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=kirc_tcga_pan_can_atlas_2018)** — 512 samples
   - mRNA Expression RSEM with z-scores

3. **[Kidney Renal Clear Cell Carcinoma (TCGA, Firehose Legacy)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=kirc_tcga)** — 538 samples
   - mRNA Expression (RNA-Seq V2 RSEM + microarray) with z-scores

4. **[Kidney Renal Clear Cell Carcinoma (TCGA, Nature 2013)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=kirc_tcga_pub)** — 446 samples
   - mRNA Expression (RNA-Seq V2 RSEM + microarray) plus miRNA expression

5. **[Metastatic Renal Clear Cell Carcinoma (Phase 1 Biomarker Trial, Clin Cancer Res. 2016) - iAtlas Harmonized](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=ccrcc_iatlas_choueiri_2016)** — 16 samples
   - mRNA Expression (log2 upper quartile normalized) with z-scores

### Papillary RCC
6. **[Papillary Renal Cell Carcinoma (TCGA GDC, 2025)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=prcc_tcga_gdc)** — 292 samples
   - mRNA Expression (FPKM, TPM, counts) with z-scores

7. **[Kidney Renal Papillary Cell Carcinoma (TCGA, PanCancer Atlas)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=kirp_tcga_pan_can_atlas_2018)** — 283 samples
   - mRNA Expression RSEM with z-scores

8. **[Kidney Renal Papillary Cell Carcinoma (TCGA, Firehose Legacy)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=kirp_tcga)** — 293 samples
   - mRNA Expression (RNA-Seq V2 RSEM + microarray) with z-scores

### Chromophobe RCC
9. **[Chromophobe Renal Cell Carcinoma (TCGA GDC, 2025)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=chrcc_tcga_gdc)** — 66 samples
   - mRNA Expression (FPKM, TPM, counts) with z-scores

10. **[Kidney Chromophobe (TCGA, PanCancer Atlas)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=kich_tcga_pan_can_atlas_2018)** — 283 samples
    - mRNA Expression RSEM with z-scores

11. **[Kidney Chromophobe (TCGA, Firehose Legacy)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=kich_tcga)** — 293 samples
    - mRNA Expression (RNA-Seq V2 RSEM) with z-scores

### Clinical Trial RCC
12. **[Renal Cell Carcinoma (IMmotion150 Clinical Trial, Nat Med. 2018) - iAtlas Harmonized](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=rcc_iatlas_immotion150_2018)** — 263 samples
    - mRNA Expression (TPM) with z-scores

13. **[Renal Cell Carcinoma (CPTAC GDC, 2025)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=rcc_cptac_gdc)** — 354 samples
    - mRNA Expression (FPKM, TPM, counts) with z-scores

**Note:** The TCGA 2025 GDC updates (ccrcc_tcga_gdc, prcc_tcga_gdc, chrcc_tcga_gdc, rcc_cptac_gdc) are the most current versions and are recommended for new analyses.
