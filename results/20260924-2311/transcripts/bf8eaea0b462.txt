## [MSK-CHORD NSCLC Study Overview](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=msk_chord_2024&filterAttributeId=CANCER_TYPE&filterValues=Non-Small+Cell+Lung+Cancer)

### Study Information
- **Study ID:** `msk_chord_2024`
- **Total MSK-CHORD cohort:** 25,040 samples from 24,950 patients
- **NSCLC subset:** 7,809 patients with Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer
- **Data type:** Targeted sequencing via MSK-IMPACT panels (multiple versions: 341–505 genes)

### Key Features
- **Comprehensive treatment data:** chemotherapy, immunotherapy, targeted therapy, hormone therapy, and radiation
- **Clinical annotations:** including NLP-derived data
- **License:** Creative Commons BY-NC-ND 4.0 (contact datarequests@mskcc.org for commercial use)
- **Sample types:** Primary tumors, metastases, local recurrences

### Download Options

1. **In cBioPortal (linked above):**
   - Click the **Download** tab to export mutation, CNA, and clinical data
   - Filter by CANCER_TYPE = "Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer" to get NSCLC-only data
   - Export as tab-separated or comma-separated files

2. **Via cBioPortal API:**
   - Documentation: https://www.cbioportal.org/api/swagger-ui.html
   - Query NSCLC samples with filters for `cancer_type=Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer`

3. **Contact MSK directly:**
   - For bulk data requests or additional formats: datarequests@mskcc.org
