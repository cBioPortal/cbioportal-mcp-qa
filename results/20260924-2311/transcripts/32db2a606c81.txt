The cBioPortal database contains a diverse range of cancer types spanning **solid tumors, hematologic malignancies, pediatric cancers, and cancer cell lines**. Here's an overview organized by category:

## **Solid Tumors**
- **Lung:** Lung adenocarcinoma (LUAD), non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC)
- **Breast:** Invasive carcinoma, metastatic breast cancer
- **Colorectal:** Colorectal adenocarcinoma, metastatic CRC
- **Pancreatic:** Pancreatic adenocarcinoma
- **Prostate:** Prostate adenocarcinoma
- **Bladder:** Urothelial carcinoma
- **Renal:** Renal cell carcinoma (clear cell)
- **Hepatic:** Hepatocellular carcinoma, hepatobiliary cancers
- **Ovarian:** High-grade serous ovarian cancer
- **Endometrial:** Uterine corpus endometrial carcinoma
- **Esophageal/Gastric:** Esophagogastric cancer, stomach cancer
- **Head & Neck:** Adenoid cystic carcinoma
- **Sarcoma:** Soft tissue and bone sarcoma
- **Melanoma:** Cutaneous melanoma

## **Hematologic Malignancies**
- Acute myeloid leukemia (AML)
- Acute lymphoid leukemia (ALL)
- Chronic lymphocytic leukemia (CLL)
- Diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL)
- Myelodysplastic syndromes (MDS)
- Histiocytic and dendritic cell neoplasms

## **Central Nervous System**
- Glioblastoma multiforme (GBM)
- Diffuse glioma (LGG)

## **Pediatric Cancers**
- Neuroblastoma, Wilms' tumor, and pediatric leukemias/lymphomas from TARGET consortium

## **Multi-Cancer Cohorts**
- **MSK-IMPACT** (50K samples, 2026) — broad clinical sequencing cohort
- **MSK-CHORD** (25K samples) — hereditary cancer study
- **Pan-cancer studies** (TCGA, PCAWG, China OrigiMed, HCMI organoids)

## **Cancer Cell Lines**
- CCLE (Broad, 1980+ cell lines)

Most studies include **mutation, copy number, and clinical data**; larger cohorts have **expression, protein, and methylation profiling**. The database emphasizes recent large cohorts (especially MSK), TCGA studies, and emerging pediatric initiatives.

Browse the full list at [cBioPortal Studies](https://www.cbioportal.org).
