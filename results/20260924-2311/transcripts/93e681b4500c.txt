**No, cBioPortal does not currently have studies with polygenic risk scores (PRS).**

I searched the database across all clinical attributes and genetic profiles. The closest data available is:

- **Genetic Ancestry** — available across 31 TCGA PanCancer Atlas studies (ACC, BLCA, BRCA, CESC, CHOL, COADREAD, DLBC, ESCA, GBM, HNSC, KICH, KIRC, KIRP, LGG, LIHC, LUAD, LUSC, MESO, OV, PAAD, PCPG, PRAD, SARC, SKCM, STAD, TGCT, THCA, THYM, UCEC, UCS, UVM). This reflects ancestry/ethnicity labels, not quantitative PRS values.

If you're interested in working with PRS data, you would need to:
1. Source the PRS scores from a study's external publications or supplementary data
2. Contact the cBioPortal team about adding PRS data to a study you're working with
