Great news! **Both lower grade glioma studies have race and ethnicity data:**

1. **[Brain Lower Grade Glioma (TCGA, Firehose Legacy)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=lgg_tcga)** (`lgg_tcga`)
   - 530 samples
   - Includes: RACE and ETHNICITY

2. **[Brain Lower Grade Glioma (TCGA, PanCancer Atlas)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=lgg_tcga_pan_can_atlas_2018)** (`lgg_tcga_pan_can_atlas_2018`)
   - 514 samples
   - Includes: RACE and ETHNICITY

The PanCancer Atlas version is generally preferred for new analyses (better harmonization), but both studies contain the demographic data you're looking for. You can explore the racial/ethnic distribution in the **Clinical Data** tab on either study's Summary page.
