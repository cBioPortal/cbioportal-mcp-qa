## Pediatric Cancer Studies Published in the Last 5 Years (2021–2026)

Here are the pediatric cancer studies in cBioPortal that were published or added since September 2021:

### 2024–2025 Publications

1. **[Pediatric Acute Myeloid Leukemia (St. Jude, Nat Genet 2024)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=aml_stjude_2024)** — 887 samples
   - RNA-Seq, WES, and WGS profiling of pediatric AML cases with matched normals

2. **[Pediatric Neuroblastoma (MSK, Nat Genet 2023)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=nbl_msk_2023)** — 223 samples
   - Targeted MSK-IMPACT sequencing of pediatric neuroblastoma tumor/normal pairs

3. **[Pediatric solid tumor PDXs (UTHSA, Nat Commun 2023)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=pancan_pdx_uthsa_2023)** — 136 samples
   - WGS/WES of patient-derived xenografts and paired pediatric patient tumors

4. **[Pediatric Rhabdomyosarcomas (MSK, JCO Precis Oncol 2023)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=rms_msk_2023)** — 24 samples
   - Targeted MSK-IMPACT sequencing of extremity rhabdomyosarcoma with matched normals

5. **[Pediatric Tumors (MSK, 2025)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=pancan_ped_mai_msk_2025)** — 60 samples
   - Targeted MSK-IMPACT sequencing of mixed pediatric solid and CNS tumors (Make An Impact program)

6. **[Pediatric Low-Grade Glioma (CTF, Acta Neuropathol 2021)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=lgg_ctf_synodos_2025)** — 31 samples
   - WGS of low-grade gliomas with neurofibromatosis; NF Open Science Initiative reprocessing

### 2022 Publications

7. **[Pediatric European MAPPYACTS Trial (Gustave Roussy, Cancer Discov 2022)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=pancan_mappyacts_2022)** — 786 samples
   - WES of multi-cancer pediatric cohort with actionable mutation annotation

8. **[Pediatric Pancan Tumors (MSK, Nat Commun 2022)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=mixed_kunga_msk_2022)** — 135 samples
   - Targeted NGS of mixed pediatric tumors via MSK-IMPACT

---

**Note:** Older TARGET (2018) studies and studies from 2020–2021 (CPTAC brain cancer) are also available but published before the 5-year window. All studies include comprehensive genomic profiling suitable for biomedical research into pediatric cancer biology and treatment development.
