I can't download files directly from here. The portal can export the NSCLC subset for you, though. I've opened the MSK-CHORD (MSK, Nature 2024) study with a Cancer Type = Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer filter applied.

**Subset size:** 7,809 NSCLC samples, out of 25,040 samples in the full study.

[View MSK-CHORD NSCLC clinical data table](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/clinicalData?id=msk_chord_2024#filterJson=%7B%22clinicalDataFilters%22%3A%5B%7B%22attributeId%22%3A%22CANCER_TYPE%22%2C%22values%22%3A%5B%7B%22value%22%3A%22Non-Small%20Cell%20Lung%20Cancer%22%7D%5D%7D%5D%7D)

**To download the NSCLC subset:**
1. On that page, use the download icon at the top right of the clinical data table. It exports the filtered samples' clinical data as a text file.
2. To get the mutation, copy-number and structural-variant data for the same samples, go to the [study summary page](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=msk_chord_2024) and apply the Cancer Type filter there. Then choose "Download" from the top-right menu, or use the selected-samples option to save the cohort.

**To get the whole study:** the full MSK-CHORD dataset is available from the cBioPortal [Datasets page](https://www.cbioportal.org/datasets) as `msk_chord_2024.tar.gz`. You can then filter it to NSCLC offline using the `CANCER_TYPE` column in the clinical sample file.
