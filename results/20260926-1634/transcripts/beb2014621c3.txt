## Major Cancer Type Categories

**Adrenal/Endocrine:**

- Anal Cancer, Appendiceal Cancer

**Genitourinary:**
- Bladder/Urinary Tract Cancer

**Breast & Gynecologic:**
- Breast Cancer (multiple subtypes: IDC, ILC, inflammatory, metaplastic, etc.)
- Ovarian Cancer, Uterine Cancer
- Cervical Cancer, Vaginal Cancer
- Endometrial Cancer

**CNS/Brain:**
- Glioblastoma, Glioma (multiple grades and molecular subtypes)
- Medulloblastoma, Ependymoma
- Meningioma, Brain Stem Glioma
- Embryonal Tumors

**Head & Neck:**
- Oral/Oropharyngeal Squamous Cell Carcinoma
- Laryngeal Cancer, Salivary Gland Cancer
- Thyroid Cancer (papillary, follicular, anaplastic)

**Lung:**
- Lung Adenocarcinoma, Lung Squamous Cell Carcinoma
- Small Cell Lung Cancer

**Hematologic:**
- Acute Myeloid Leukemia, Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia
- Chronic Myeloid Leukemia, Chronic Lymphocytic Leukemia
- Hodgkin Lymphoma, Non-Hodgkin Lymphoma
- Multiple Myeloma

**Musculoskeletal:**
- Osteosarcoma, Ewing Sarcoma, Chondrosarcoma
- Bone Sarcoma (various subtypes)

**Skin:**
- Melanoma (cutaneous, uveal, mucosal)
- Merkel Cell Carcinoma, Basal Cell Carcinoma

**Soft Tissue & Other:**
- Angiosarcoma, Mesothelioma
- Neurofibromatosis-associated tumors
- Pediatric cancers (TARGET)
