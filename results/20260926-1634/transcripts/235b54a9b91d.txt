## Pediatric Cancer Studies (Published 2021–2026)

Here are the **pediatric cancer studies** in cBioPortal **published in the last 5 years** (2021–2026), sorted by publication year:

### **2025**
1. [Pediatric Tumors (MSK, 2025)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=pancan_ped_mai_msk_2025) — 60 samples from the Make An Impact (MAI) program via MSK-IMPACT

### **2024**
2. [Pediatric Acute Myeloid Leukemia (St. Jude, Nat Genet 2024)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=aml_stjude_2024) — 887 pediatric AML cases with RNA-Seq, WES, WGS

### **2023**
3. [Pediatric Neuroblastoma (MSK, Nat Genet 2023)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=nbl_msk_2023) — 223 neuroblastoma tumor/normal pairs via MSK-IMPACT
4. [Pediatric solid tumor PDXs (UTHSA, Nat Commun 2023)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=pancan_pdx_uthsa_2023) — 68 PDXs + 65 patient tumors (136 samples)
5. [Pediatric Rhabdomyosarcomas (MSK, JCO Precis Oncol 2023)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=rms_msk_2023) — 24 rhabdomyosarcoma cases via MSK-IMPACT

### **2022**
6. [Pediatric Pancan Tumors (MSK, Nat Commun 2022)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=mixed_kunga_msk_2022) — 135 pediatric mixed tumors via MSK-IMPACT
7. [Pediatric European MAPPYACTS Trial (Gustave Roussy, Cancer Discov 2022)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=pancan_mappyacts_2022) — 786 pediatric samples with actionable mutations

### **2020–2021**
8. [Pediatric Brain Cancer (CPTAC/CHOP, Cell 2020)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=brain_cptac_2020) — 218 pediatric brain tumors (7 histological types) with proteogenomics
9. [Pediatric Low-Grade Glioma (CTF, Acta Neuropathol 2021)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=lgg_ctf_synodos_2025) — 31 pediatric LGG samples with neurofibromatosis

**Total: 9 studies** published in the last 5 years, spanning pediatric leukemia, solid tumors, brain tumors, and PDX models.
