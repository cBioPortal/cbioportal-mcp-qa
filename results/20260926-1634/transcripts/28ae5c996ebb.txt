MSK-CHORD (Nature 2024) is a pan-cancer cohort of 25,040 samples spanning NSCLC, breast, colorectal, prostate, and pancreatic cancer — there's no separate NSCLC-only study ID, so this link opens the full study pre-filtered to just the **Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer** cancer type.

**To download the data:**
1. Open the link above — it loads with the NSCLC filter already applied.
2. Click **"Selected patients/samples"** at the top of the page, or check the filtered summary to confirm the subset.
3. Use the **Download** icon/tab on the study page to export clinical data, mutations, copy-number alterations, or structural variants for just that filtered NSCLC cohort (available profiles: mutations, CNA, structural variants).

If you'd rather script the download, the same filtered data is accessible via the public [cBioPortal REST API](https://www.cbioportal.org/api) using study ID `msk_chord_2024` with an `ONCOTREE_CODE`/`CANCER_TYPE` clinical filter for NSCLC — happy to help draft that query if useful.
