Yes, **cBioPortal contains imaging data**. The database includes links to multiple types of imaging and pathology images through the `resource_definition`, `resource_patient`, and `resource_sample` tables:

### Available Imaging Types

**Medical Imaging (via IDC/OHIF viewer):**
- **Computed Tomography (CT)**
- **Magnetic Resonance (MR)**
- **Positron Emission Tomography (PET)**
- **Nuclear Medicine (NM)**
- **Mammography (MG)**
- **Radiography** (Computed Radiography CR, Digital Radiography DX)

**Microscopy & Pathology:**
- **H&E Slides** — Histopathology images
- **CyCIF** — Cyclic Immunofluorescence multiplexed images
- **Slide Microscopy (IDC_SLIM)** — Whole-slide microscopy
- **MxIF Images** — Multiplexed immunofluorescence
- **Chromoscope** — Interactive virtual microscopy

### How It Works

These resources are **linked at the patient or sample level** through:
- `resource_patient` table — patient-level imaging (most imaging data)
- `resource_sample` table — sample-level pathology images (H&E, CyCIF, MxIF)

When you view a patient or sample in PatientView, cBioPortal displays clickable links to these images in an external viewer (typically the **IDC OHIF Viewer** for medical images or **Minerva** for whole-slide pathology).
