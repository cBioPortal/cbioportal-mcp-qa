═ answer (success)
Yes, there are **two lower grade glioma studies with race data**:

1. **[Brain Lower Grade Glioma (TCGA, Firehose Legacy)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=lgg_tcga)** (`lgg_tcga`)
   - 530 total samples
   - **505 patients with race data**

2. **[Brain Lower Grade Glioma (TCGA, PanCancer Atlas)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=lgg_tcga_pan_can_atlas_2018)** (`lgg_tcga_pan_can_atlas_2018`)
   - 514 total samples
   - **503 patients with race data**

Both studies have comprehensive race category data. The PanCancer Atlas version is the more recent standardized release and is generally preferred for analyses.
