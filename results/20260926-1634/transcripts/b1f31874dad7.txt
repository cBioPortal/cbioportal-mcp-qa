### **How to Access the Raw Data Files:**

These studies are public, so you can:
1. Check the official cBioPortal GitHub repository: `https://github.com/cBioPortal/datahub`
2. Look in the respective study directories (e.g., `studies/acc_2019/`) for:
   - `meta_clinical_outcomes.txt` (metadata file)
   - `data_clinical_outcomes.txt` (actual data)

Alternatively, check the publication's supplementary materials or contact the study authors' institutions (many MSK and TCGA studies make raw files openly available).

### **Key File Components to Emulate:**

**`meta_clinical_outcomes.txt`** should specify:
- `datatype: STRING` or `NUMBER` 
- `attribute_type: STRING` (for STATUS fields)
- `description`, `display_name`
- `priority` (sort order in the portal)

**`data_clinical_outcomes.txt`** should have:
- Column headers: `PATIENT_ID`, `SAMPLE_ID`, `OS_STATUS`, `OS_MONTHS`, `DFS_STATUS`, `DFS_MONTHS`, etc.
- Status as `0:LIVING, 1:DECEASED` or `0:EVENT_FREE, 1:WITH_EVENT`
- Months as numeric values
