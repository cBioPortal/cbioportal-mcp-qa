- **Cholangiocarcinoma:** IDH1 mutations are uncommon overall (~5–10%), but when IDH1 is hit it is almost exclusively **R132H**, most commonly in the intrahepatic subtype.

## On chondrosarcoma — a data-source caveat

This one is worth being careful about: chondrosarcoma does **not** carry IDH1 mutations in the somatic sense that glioma does.
