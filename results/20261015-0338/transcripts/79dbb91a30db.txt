Q (qwen3.8-27b): In the MSK-CHORD study, is it possible to see which patients received radiation therapy?

═ answer (success)
Yes — treatment and therapy information (including whether patients received radiation therapy) can be found in the **Clinical Data** section of the MSK-CHORD study. The specific radiation-related attributes are:

- `RADIATION_THERAPY_YN_1` — whether radiation therapy was given (e.g., "Yes" / "No")
- `RADIATION_THERAPY_YN_1_TIME` — the time of first radiation-therapy assessment
- `RADIATION_THERAPY_YN_2` — whether radiation therapy was given (later assessment)
- `RADIATION_THERAPY_YN_2_TIME` — the time of second radiation-therapy assessment
- `RADIATION_THERAPY_TYPE_1`, `RADIATION_THERAPY_TYPE_2` — the type of radiation therapy (e.g., "Radiation Therapy", "Other", "NA")
- Plus various "TUMOR SITE X"-specific radiation attributes and `RADIATION_THERAPY_YN_1_TYPE_1`
- Related treatment records also appear as clinical *events* (treatments), including entries labeled "Radiographic Therapy"/radiation for many individual tumor sites

You can view these directly in the study:

[MSK-CHORD (2024) — Clinical Data tab](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=msk_chord_2024&tab=clinicalData)

On that page, use the **Add attribute** picker to add any of the `RADIATION_THERAPY_*` attributes as a chart; each plot shows how many patients fall into each category (e.g., "Yes" vs "No"). To list which *individual patients* received radiation, add the `RADIATION_THERAPY_YN_1` attribute, filter the cohort to `Yes`, then click a patient to open their full profile in PatientView (their Clinical Data / Clinical timeline will show the radiation records with dates and types).

A couple of notes:
- cBioPortal shows the raw recorded values, so "Yes" / "No" / "Unknown"/"NA" will all appear depending on what was recorded per patient.
- This reflects what each study recorded at MSKCC; it's research-registered data, not a complete clinical record.
