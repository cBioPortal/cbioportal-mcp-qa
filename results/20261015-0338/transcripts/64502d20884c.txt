**[IDH1 mutant vs EGFR-amplified — survival curves, LGG (TCGA PanCancer Atlas, n=514)](https://www.cbioportal.org/comparison/survival?comparisonId=6ad08408c2115c492d888409)**

- **IDH1 Mutant group:** 395 of 514 samples (77%)
- **EGFR Amplified group:** 39 of 514 samples (8%)

A few caveats on how this comparison is set up — worth stating plainly before you read anything into it:
- **These are two partially overlapping gene-defined populations, not a mutually exclusive 2×2 partition.** IDH1-mutant and EGFR-amplified are *not* cleanly opposite categories — a meaningful subset of LGGs are IDH1-mutant **and** EGFR-amplified (EGFR amplification is enriched in the IDH1-mutant, no-1p/19q-codeleted "astrotic" subgroup). So the "difference" you'll see here is a gene-by-gene comparison, not a clean head-to-head of two disjoint groups. If you want a true contrast, the classic LGG question is **IDH1-mutant + 1p/19q-codeleted (oligodendroglioma) vs IDH1-wildtype**, or **IDH1-mutant ± EGFR amp**.
- I defined "amplified" using cBioPortal GISTIC **AMP = copy-number value 2** (39 samples). EGFR *Gain* (value 1) accounts for another 87 samples; let me know if you'd like to broaden to Gain+AMP.
- I can't compute a hazard ratio or p-value from here — that needs Kaplan–Meier + log-rank, which the Survival tab does for you (use the OS_MONTHS / OS_STATUS pair by default in that study).
