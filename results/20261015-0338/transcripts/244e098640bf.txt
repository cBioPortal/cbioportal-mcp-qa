## What the nomenclature means

**`p.*1069Wext*3`** is a HGVS protein-level (c.1069) variant notation:

| Part | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `p.*1069` | The alteration involves the 1069-position of the canonical/reference PIK3CA protein (the `p.*` here refers to the stop/codon-position context in the extended notation) |
| `W` | Tryptophan (Trp) at the affected codon |
| `ext*3` | **e**xtension of **3** amino acids before the stop codon — i.e., the normal terminating codon has been altered so that translation runs *through* the normal stop and inserts 3 additional amino acids (Lys-Arg-…) before a new stop codon is encountered |
| `*3` | The three extra residues are encoded by the codons immediately downstream of the normal stop |

So this is a **stop-gained / frameshift-class extension at the C-terminus of PIK3CA (a p.Arg1069*-type extension)**, i.e. a small C-terminal insertion beyond codon 1069. In HGVS this would correspond to something like `p.[Trp1069*ext]` — I have NOT verified the exact protein sequence of PIK3CA residue 1069 in this database (cBioPortal does not store per-residue sequences), so take the residue identity (W) from your report's source; I'm interpreting it purely from the HGVS syntax you gave me.

## Important caveats before reading this as oncogenic

1. **It is at the extreme C-terminus.** PIK3CA (p110α) has 964 amino acids in the canonical UniProt sequence — a "position 1069" refers to the nucleotide-based c.1069 convention of the coding sequence / transcript, not an amino-acid position in the 964-residue protein. This is a common source of confusion in reports; please confirm with your reporting lab whether `*1069` is a **cDNA (c.1069)** coordinate or a protein coordinate, because the two produce different biological consequences.
   - If it is **c.1069** (DNA/RNA coordinate): this is in the **kinase domain** of PIK3CA, near the end of the coding sequence, and an `ext*3` at that location is the well-characterized recurrent activating class (the canonical PIK3CA H1047R mutation is the most common activating variant).
   - If it is a **protein coordinate beyond residue 964**, the notation would be unusual and the report may be using extended (alternate-isoform) numbering.

2. **`ext*3` (extended stop) at the very C-terminus of PIK3CA** — this exact class is *not* one of the classic hot-spot mutations (H1047R, E542K, E545K) that define PIK3CA-activating mutations in cBioPortal data. Most `ext` / frameshift / nonsense truncations at the C-terminus are treated as **likely loss-of-function or neutral/uncertain**, not activating, because they often trigger NMD (nonsense-mediated mRNA decay) or are tolerated at the tail. **Do not assume this is an oncogenic driver.** cBioPortal does not store OncoKB/CancerHotspots annotations, so I cannot confirm driver significance for this specific codon from the database — please cross-check in OncoKB (pi_k3ca > HUGO:PIK3CA) and ClinVar.
