I can't confirm which study has a file named `data_clinical_outcomes.txt`. The database stores clinical attributes, not the names of the files they were loaded from. Also, cBioPortal's standard study format uses `data_clinical_patient.txt` and `data_clinical_sample.txt`, so that filename may come from a custom or non-portal source. The closest proxy is studies with patient-level outcome fields (OS, DFS, PFS, DSS, RFS), which is typically what an outcomes file contains.

Studies with at least two outcome fields, largest first:

- **[Breast Invasive Carcinoma (TCGA, PanCancer Atlas)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=brca_tcga_pan_can_atlas_2018)**: 1,084 samples. OS, DFS, DSS, PFS.
- **[Colorectal Adenocarcinoma (TCGA, PanCancer Atlas)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=coadread_tcga_pan_can_atlas_2018)**: 594 samples. OS, DFS, DSS, PFS.
- **[Glioblastoma Multiforme (TCGA, PanCancer Atlas)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=gbm_tcga_pan_can_atlas_2018)**: 592 samples. OS, DFS, DSS, PFS.
- **[Breast Cancer (METABRIC, Nature 2012 & Nat Commun 2016)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=brca_metabric)**: 2,509 samples. OS, RFS.
- **[MSK-IMPACT 50K Clinical Sequencing Cohort (MSK, Cancer Cell 2026)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=msk_impact_50k_2026)**: 54,331 samples. OS only.

The TCGA PanCancer Atlas studies have the most complete outcome sets, so they're the strongest candidates for emulation. The MSK-IMPACT studies are much larger but have only OS.

To check the actual file names, look in the study folder of the cBioPortal datahub on GitHub (`cbioportal/datahub`, `public/<study_id>/`). That's the only place to confirm whether a `data_clinical_outcomes.txt` exists. If you have a specific study ID in mind, I can check its outcome fields and clinical attributes in more detail.
